Our Story

Three ovens, one starter, no shortcuts.

Wildflour started as a single deck oven in a garage and a sourdough culture named Gerald, who is now older than most of our regulars' kids. Everything we bake still runs through his descendants.

We mill a portion of our own flour, proof everything overnight, and bake in small batches so nothing sits under a heat lamp waiting to be sold.

  • Flour milled in-house, twice weekly
  • 24-hour cold ferment on every loaf
  • Butter-forward laminated pastry, folded by hand
